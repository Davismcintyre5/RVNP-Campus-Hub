1. ABSTRACT

RVNP Campus Hub is a comprehensive, all-in-one social and campus management platform designed specifically for Rift Valley National Polytechnic (RVNP). The software unifies the core features of Facebook, Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, and Telegram into a single platform tailored for an educational institution. It connects all five RVNP campuses — Main Campus, Nakuru City Campus, Kericho Campus, Mwachon Campus, and Kureisoi Campus — into one digital community.

The platform serves three main purposes: (1) marketing Rift Valley National Polytechnic to prospective students and the wider public, (2) sharing official information instantly from administration to students and staff, and (3) building an engaged campus community across all campuses. It additionally integrates an artificial intelligence assistant called HDM AI that functions as a school assistant, capable of answering questions about the institution from guests, students, and staff.

The software is built using a modern MERN stack with PostgreSQL: React 18 with Vite and Tailwind CSS for the frontend, Node.js with Express for the backend API, PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM for the database, Redis for caching and sessions, Cloudinary for media storage, and Socket.io for real-time communication. It is deployed as a Progressive Web Application (PWA) accessible from any modern browser, and it supports mobile and desktop experiences through responsive design.

The system incorporates fifteen major feature modules: authentication, social feed, reels, stories, messaging, groups, events, marketplace, notifications, search, privacy controls, hashtags, mentions, badges, leaderboards, and a full administrative dashboard. Each module is designed with strict role-based access control supporting Students, Staff, Alumni, Guests, Admins, and Super Admins.

2. INTRODUCTION

2.1 Problem Statement

Educational institutions such as Rift Valley National Polytechnic face persistent challenges in digital communication and community engagement. Official information is scattered across multiple channels including notice boards, WhatsApp groups, email, and social media pages that are not institution-controlled. Students on different campuses often feel disconnected from one another, and there is no unified digital space where students, staff, and alumni can interact. Additionally, prospective students and the general public have limited direct access to accurate information about the institution, relying instead on informal sources.

Existing solutions are fragmented: Facebook and WhatsApp are generic platforms not designed for educational institutions; institutional websites are static and do not support interaction; and multiple separate tools force users to switch between apps. There is a clear need for a single, integrated platform that serves marketing, information sharing, and community engagement in one place.

2.2 Solution Overview

RVNP Campus Hub solves these problems by providing one platform that combines the best features of the most widely used social applications. From Facebook it takes posts, pages, groups, and events. From Messenger it takes direct messaging. From WhatsApp it takes status updates and simple encrypted chat. From TikTok it takes short-form vertical videos (Reels). From Telegram it takes channels and large community groups. All of these features are re-designed around the specific needs of an educational institution.

The core objectives are threefold. First, marketing: the platform publicly showcases RVNP's programs, campuses, achievements, and student life to attract new students. Second, information sharing: the administration can publish official announcements that reach every user instantly, with campus-specific and department-specific targeting. Third, community engagement: students, staff, and alumni across all five campuses can interact, join groups, share content, and build lasting connections.

Uniquely, the platform includes an AI assistant called HDM AI that functions as a 24/7 school assistant. Guests — people who are not yet registered students or staff — can chat with HDM AI to learn about RVNP's courses, campuses, admissions, and campus life. This serves the marketing objective directly, giving prospective students a friendly, always-available information channel.

3.3 Multi-Campus Architecture

The platform is designed from the ground up to support multiple campuses under a single unified system. Five campuses are seeded: Main Campus (Njoro Road, Nakuru), Nakuru City Campus, Kericho Campus, Mwachon Campus, and Kureisoi Campus. Each user is associated with a campus through a foreign key. Campus-scoped content includes posts, reels, groups, events, and stories. Users can view content from their own campus via the "My Campus" tab in the feed, while the "All Posts" tab shows content from the entire institution.

3.5 HDM AI Architecture

HDM AI is integrated through a single general chat API endpoint provided by HDM: POST https://hdmaiserver.pxxl.click/api/v1/projects/general/public-chat. All AI features use this single endpoint with different system prompts. Three AI features are supported:

FeatureSystem Prompt PurposeAdmin Toggle
Chat AssistantFriendly campus AI assistant for RVNP questions and general conversationChat Enabled
Content GeneratorContent writer for posts, reel captions, and eventsContent Enabled
Comment AnalysisAnalyzer that returns JSON with summary, sentiment, key topics, and notable commentsComment Analysis Enabled

When AI is enabled by admin, a special AI user with isAI = true is created or updated. This user appears in the Messages list with HDM Verified badge and is always online. Guests and users can chat with HDM AI at /messages/ai.

3.6 Real-Time Architecture

Real-time communication is powered by Socket.io. When a user connects, the socket joins three rooms: user:{userId}, campus:{campusId}, and group:{groupId}. The server emits the following events: new-message for chat, notification for in-app notifications, campus-notification for campus-wide alerts, and group-notification for group events. The frontend NotificationContext listens to these events and updates unread counts and notification lists in real time.

5.1 Registration with OTP and Email/SMS Verification

// controllers/client/authController.js
const register = asyncHandler(async (req, res) => {
  const { fullName, email, phoneNumber, password, role, campusId, ... } = req.body;

  const userRole = ['STUDENT', 'STAFF', 'ALUMNI'].includes(role) ? role : 'STUDENT';

  const otp = generateOtp();
  const hashedOtp = await hashOtp(otp);
  const tempId = generateRandomToken(16);

  const pendingRegistration = {
    fullName, email, phoneNumber, password, role: userRole,
    campusId, departmentId, course, yearOfStudy, hashedOtp,
    otpExpiresAt: generateTokenExpiry(env.otp.expiresIn),
  };

  await cacheService.setCache(`reg:${tempId}`, pendingRegistration, env.otp.expiresIn);

  await emailService.sendVerificationEmail({ to: email, fullName, otp });
  await smsService.sendOtpSMS({ to: phoneNumber, otp });

  res.json(ApiResponse.ok({ tempId, message: 'OTP sent successfully' }));
});

const verifyRegistration = asyncHandler(async (req, res) => {
  const { tempId, otp } = req.body;
  const pending = await cacheService.getCache(`reg:${tempId}`);
  const isValid = await verifyOtp(otp, pending.hashedOtp);

  const passwordHash = await hashPassword(pending.password);
  const user = await prisma.user.create({ data: { ...pending, passwordHash, verificationStatus: 'VERIFIED' } });

  await emailService.sendWelcomeEmail({ to: user.email, fullName: user.fullName });

  res.status(201).json(ApiResponse.created({ user, accessToken, refreshToken }));
});

5.2 Real-Time Chat with Delivery Ticks

// controllers/client/messageController.js
const sendMessage = asyncHandler(async (req, res) => {
  const { conversationId } = req.params;
  const { content, type } = req.body;
  const userId = req.user.id;

  const conversation = await Conversation.findById(conversationId);
  const otherParticipant = conversation.participants.find((p) => p.userId !== userId);

  const message = await Message.create({
    conversationId,
    senderId: userId,
    recipientId: otherParticipant?.userId || null,
    content,
    type: type || 'TEXT',
    deliveredAt: new Date(),
  });

  await Conversation.updateLastMessage(conversationId, message.id);

  if (otherParticipant) {
    socketService.notifyNewMessage(otherParticipant.userId, message);
  }

  res.status(201).json(ApiResponse.created(message));
});

5.3 HDM AI Integration (Single Endpoint, Multiple Prompts)

// config/hdmai.js
const hdmai = {
  callChatAPI: async (message, systemPrompt) => {
    const config = await hdmai.getConfig();
    const endpoint = `${config.baseUrl}/projects/general/public-chat`;

    const response = await axios.post(endpoint, { message, system_prompt: systemPrompt }, {
      headers: { Authorization: `Bearer ${config.apiKey}`, 'Content-Type': 'application/json' },
      timeout: 30000,
    });

    return response.data.data;
  },

  chat: async (message) => {
    const config = await hdmai.getConfig();
    const systemPrompt = `You are ${config.name}, a friendly campus AI assistant for RVNP Campus Hub...`;
    const result = await hdmai.callChatAPI(message, systemPrompt);
    return { reply: result.reply, provider: result.provider };
  },

  generateContent: async (prompt, type = 'post') => {
    let systemPrompt = '';
    switch (type) {
      case 'post': systemPrompt = `You are a content writer for RVNP Campus Hub...`; break;
      case 'reel_caption': systemPrompt = `You are a caption writer for RVNP Campus Hub...`; break;
      case 'event': systemPrompt = `You are an event promoter for RVNP Campus Hub...`; break;
    }
    const result = await hdmai.callChatAPI(prompt, systemPrompt);
    return { content: result.reply };
  },
};

5.4 Post Creation with Hashtags, Mentions, and Badge Check

// controllers/client/postController.js
const createPost = asyncHandler(async (req, res) => {
  const { content, privacy, campusId } = req.body;
  const userId = req.user.id;

  const post = await Post.create({ content, privacy: privacy || 'PUBLIC', userId, campusId: campusId || req.user.campusId });

  // Extract hashtags from content and increment their post counts
  if (content?.text) {
    const hashtags = extractHashtags(content.text);
    for (const tag of hashtags) {
      await Hashtag.upsert(tag);
    }
  }

  // Check and award badges
  await Badge.checkAndAwardBadges(userId);

  res.status(201).json(ApiResponse.created(post));
});

5.6 Reaction Summary ("You, Jane, and 3 others")

// models/client/Reaction.js
const getReactionSummary = async (postId, reelId, commentId, currentUserId) => {
  const reactions = await prisma.reaction.findMany({ where: { postId }, include: { user: { select: { fullName: true } } } });
  const EMOJI_MAP = { LIKE: '👍', LOVE: '❤️', CARE: '🤗', HAHA: '😂', WOW: '😮', SAD: '😢', ANGRY: '😡' };

  const typeCounts = {};
  reactions.forEach((r) => { typeCounts[r.type] = (typeCounts[r.type] || 0) + 1; });

  const sortedTypes = Object.entries(typeCounts).sort((a, b) => b[1] - a[1]);
  const topEmojis = sortedTypes.slice(0, 2).map(([type]) => EMOJI_MAP[type]);

  const hasYou = reactions.some((r) => r.userId === currentUserId);
  const otherReactors = reactions.filter((r) => r.userId !== currentUserId);
  const topReactors = otherReactors.slice(0, 2).map((r) => r.user.fullName);

  let displayText = '';
  if (hasYou && topReactors.length === 0) displayText = 'You reacted';
  else if (hasYou && topReactors.length === 1) displayText = `You and ${topReactors[0]}`;
  else if (hasYou && topReactors.length === 2) displayText = `You, ${topReactors[0]}, and ${reactions.length - 3} others`;

  return { total: reactions.length, topEmojis, displayText };
};

5.7 Badge System with Auto-Award and Notification

// models/client/Badge.js
const awardBadge = async (userId, badgeId) => {
  const existing = await prisma.userBadge.findUnique({ where: { userId_badgeId: { userId, badgeId } } });
  if (existing) return existing;

  const userBadge = await prisma.userBadge.create({ data: { userId, badgeId }, include: { badge: true } });

  await notificationService.createNotification({
    userId,
    type: 'SYSTEM',
    title: 'New Badge Earned!',
    body: `You earned the ${userBadge.badge.icon} ${userBadge.badge.name} badge: ${userBadge.badge.description}`,
    data: { badgeId, badgeName: userBadge.badge.name },
  });

  return userBadge;
};

const checkAndAwardBadges = async (userId) => {
  const user = await prisma.user.findUnique({ where: { id: userId }, include: { _count: { select: { posts: true, reactions: true, sentMessages: true, createdGroups: true, listings: true } } } });

  if (user._count.posts >= 50) await awardBadge(userId, badgeMap['Top Contributor']);
  if (user._count.reactions >= 100) await awardBadge(userId, badgeMap['Top Fan']);
  if (user._count.sentMessages >= 500) await awardBadge(userId, badgeMap['Chatterbox']);
};

const extractHashtags = (text) => {
  if (!text) return [];
  const matches = text.match(/#[\w]+/g) || [];
  return [...new Set(matches.map((tag) => tag.replace('#', '').toLowerCase()))];
};

const extractMentions = (text) => {
  if (!text) return [];
  const matches = text.match(/@[\w]+/g) || [];
  return [...new Set(matches.map((mention) => mention.replace('@', '')))];
};

5.9 Role-Based Access Control Middleware

// middleware/admin/requireAdmin.js
const requireAdmin = async (req, res, next) => {
  const authHeader = req.headers.authorization;
  if (!authHeader || !authHeader.startsWith('Bearer ')) throw ApiError.unauthorized('No token provided');

  const token = authHeader.split(' ')[1];
  const decoded = verifyAccessToken(token);
  const user = await prisma.user.findUnique({ where: { id: decoded.id } });

  if (!user) throw ApiError.unauthorized('User not found');
  if (user.accountStatus === 'SUSPENDED') throw ApiError.forbidden('Account suspended');
  if (user.role !== 'ADMIN' && user.role !== 'SUPER_ADMIN') throw ApiError.forbidden('Admin access required');

  req.user = user;
  next();
};

6. UNIQUE FEATURES AND DIFFERENTIATORS

6.1 All-in-One Social Super App

RVNP Campus Hub is not simply a social app or a campus portal. It combines the essential features of five major platforms — Facebook (feed, pages, groups, events), Messenger (direct messaging), WhatsApp (status, simple chat), TikTok (short-form reels), and Telegram (channels, large groups) — into a single product that is re-designed for an educational institution.

6.2 Multi-Campus Unification

The platform is specifically designed for institutions with multiple campuses. All five RVNP campuses operate under one unified system with campus-specific content feeds, campus-scoped events, and campus-filtered users. This is a rare feature in generic social platforms.

6.3 HDM AI as School Assistant for Guests

Guest users can chat with HDM AI to learn about RVNP — courses, campuses, admissions, and campus life. This directly serves the marketing objective. The AI is powered by a single custom HDM AI endpoint and configured entirely from the admin dashboard. Guests do not need to register to ask questions.

6.4 Full Administrative Ecosystem

The admin dashboard is a complete software product in its own right: real-time statistics, user management, content moderation, reports handling, analytics with leaderboards, general settings, upload settings with dynamic file-size and type controls, AI settings, campuses and departments management, legal documents management, backups, and system health monitoring.

6.5 Modular Feature Gates

Multiple features are controlled through admin-only gates: Redis (enabled/disabled), Upload provider (local/cloudinary), SMS provider (HDM/Brevo), Email provider (HDM/Brevo), AI chat, AI content, AI comment analysis. This allows the platform to adapt to institutional needs without code changes.

6.6 Kenyan-Built for Kenyan Education

RVNP Campus Hub is built by a Kenyan developer (Davis Okoth, HDM) for a Kenyan institution (Rift Valley National Polytechnic). It uses green, white, and red as its native colors, aligning with RVNP's identity. It supports Kenya-specific phone-number formats, KSh currency displays, and local time zones.

RVNP Campus Hub represents a substantial original work of software development.
